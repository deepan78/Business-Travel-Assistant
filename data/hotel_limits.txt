Hotel Reimbursement Limits

The nightly hotel reimbursement cap depends on employee grade and city category.

Grade A:
- Metro: INR 4000 per night
- Other: INR 3000 per night

Grade B:
- Metro: INR 3000 per night
- Other: INR 2200 per night

Grade C:
- Metro: INR 2000 per night
- Other: INR 1500 per night

These caps include taxes.

For this exercise, the same nightly charge applies to every night of the stay.

The estimated hotel reimbursement is the lower of the actual nightly charge and the applicable nightly cap, multiplied by the number of nights.

Final payment depends on approval and document verification.
